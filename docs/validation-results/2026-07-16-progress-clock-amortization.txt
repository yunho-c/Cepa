Invariant under test
- traversal transport still emits no more than one update per 100 ms
- cancellation remains checked for every ingested entry
- the first retained entry samples the clock so one slow operation is visible
- steady fast ingestion samples at most once per 32 retained entries
- the next sample window adapts toward one entry when observed work is slow
- jwalk, getattrlistbulk, and statx share the same scheduling policy
- terminal finishing progress remains explicit and accounting is unchanged

Evidence boundary
This proves the production traversal loops perform far fewer deadline clock
reads on fast ingestion while adapting the next check for slow observed work.
It also proves accounting parity, bounded native cancellation observations, and
a production macOS WebView lifecycle. The integrated timing distributions are
noisy and traversal is flat; this is not evidence of an end-to-end scan speedup,
cold-cache performance, blocked-syscall latency, or native Windows runtime.
