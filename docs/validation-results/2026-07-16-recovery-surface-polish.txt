Scope
-----

This candidate separates primary recovery guidance from exact platform errors
for scan entry, terminal scan failure, scan-stop failure, and estimate-stop
failure. Exact diagnostics remain available under collapsed Error details. It
also keeps estimator Cancel and Close actions on one row at 620 by 480 and
observes terminal scan completion rejection before the immediate command
acknowledgement can race it into an unhandled WebView rejection.

Development-WebView inspection
------------------------------

The terminal scan, folder-picker, finishing-stop, and estimate-stop development
scenarios were inspected at 620 by 480 or 880 by 620 across both light and dark
appearance. Primary copy remained actionable and free of backend acknowledgement
vocabulary. Exact diagnostics were collapsed by default. The expanded terminal
diagnostic remained within the 620-pixel viewport width.

At 620 by 480, the estimator Cancel and Close controls shared one horizontal
24-pixel-high row with a 4-pixel gap, the recovery message and Error details
affordance remained visible, and the document had no horizontal overflow.
Temporary screenshots were removed after inspection.
