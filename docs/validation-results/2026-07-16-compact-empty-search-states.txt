Scope
-----

This candidate removes desktop minimum heights that could place empty-folder or
search recovery content outside the clipped directory pane at the supported
620 by 480 window minimum. The production WebView smoke now performs a real
zero-match backend search and requires both the resulting panel and its Clear
search control to be fully contained by that pane.
